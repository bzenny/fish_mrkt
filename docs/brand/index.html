05 / Standards

Keep the mark consistent.

Do

Use approved proportions, readable contrast, unaltered typography and sufficient whitespace.

Don't

Stretch, recolor arbitrarily, add competing effects or place the dark navy mark on low-contrast backgrounds.

Product language

FI$H is virtual capital. FI$H_usd is the default denomination. Never imply redeemability or guaranteed returns.
